# Fluent Emoji assets

The Deep Sea and Cosmic Mine Islands skins include unmodified SVG assets
from [Microsoft Fluent Emoji](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji)
at commit `62ecdc0d7ca5c6df32148c169556bc8d3782fca4`.

Microsoft Fluent Emoji is licensed under the MIT License. A copy is included
in `FLUENT_EMOJI_LICENSE.txt`.

## Deep Sea

- Hazard: Blowfish
- Flag: Anchor
- Triggered hazard: Shark
- Incorrect flag: Cross mark

## Cosmic

- Hazard: Flying saucer
- Flag: Satellite
- Triggered hazard: Comet
- Incorrect flag: Cross mark
